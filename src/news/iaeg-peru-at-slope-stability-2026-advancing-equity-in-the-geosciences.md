At IAEG Peru, we reaffirm our commitment to advancing engineering geology and the sustainable development of infrastructure. As part of this effort, we were glad to support the panel discussion on Women in Engineering and Slope Stability, working hand in hand with initiatives such as Women in Mine Stability (WIM) and international committees such as IAEG WEG and ABGE (the Brazilian Association of Engineering and Environmental Geology), to promote greater visibility, leadership and equity in the geotechnical and mining sectors.

We sincerely thank everyone who visited our stand and joined us for these valuable days of networking and collaborative learning.

We keep building toward a more inclusive, high-standard geotechnical community!
