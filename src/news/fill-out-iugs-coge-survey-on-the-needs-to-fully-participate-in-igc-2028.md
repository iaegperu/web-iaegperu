The International Geological Congress (IGC 2028 Canada) represents one of the world's most prominent scientific gatherings in geosciences. This survey looks at gather LAC region perspectives on:

* Access to funding and travel grants
* Barriers to international participation
* Scientific topics of interest for IGC 2028
* Opportunities for students and early-career professionals
* Strategies to strengthen regional representation

🕒 Estimated time: 5–8 minutes

📅 Updated survey deadline: Extended to October 31, 2026

🔗 Participate by scanning the QR code on the flyer

📢 We invite the entire geoscience community across Latin America and the Caribbean to fill out this survey! Your input will contribute to a COGE technical report to be shared with the IGC 2028 organizing committee to inform future decision-making.
