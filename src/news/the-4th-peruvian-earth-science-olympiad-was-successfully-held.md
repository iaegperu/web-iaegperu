With great joy, we share the results of the 4OPG, which brought together secondary students from different regions of Peru passionate about Earth Sciences.

We congratulate all participants for their effort and curiosity, as well as the teachers, families, and educational institutions that supported them. You prove that Peru has tremendous talent for geosciences 💪


We extend our gratitude to CIGECC, IUGS-COGE, partner institutions, and General Coordinator MSc. Karla Tene, who led a motivated team of professionals that made this edition possible. See you at the 5th Peruvian Earth Science Olympiad! 🚀
