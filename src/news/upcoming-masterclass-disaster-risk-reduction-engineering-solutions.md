We are pleased to share an upcoming Masterclass organized by **UNI-FIGMM**, bringing together academic expertise and practical experience in disaster risk reduction and engineering geology.

**Our President, Dr. Sandra Villacorta**, will represent the Peruvian Group of IAEG as keynote speaker, presenting:

**“Debris Flow Disaster Prevention in Peru: From Japanese Sabo Technology to Nature-Based Solutions in Rayos de Sol (Chosica)”**

The Masterclass will explore how lessons from Japan’s **Sabo technology** can be combined with innovative **Nature-Based Solutions** to strengthen debris-flow risk reduction in Peru.

📅 **September 4, 2026**
🕖 **7:00 PM (Peru Time)**
💻 **Google Meet** – link to register here.

**Moderator:** MSc. Ruth Norabuena, Professor at UNI-FIGMM

Organized by **UNI-FIGMM**, with the support of the **Peruvian Group of IAEG, ISRM and IUGS-COGE**.

👉 **Join us and discover how engineering, geosciences and nature-based approaches can work together to reduce disaster risk in Peru.**
