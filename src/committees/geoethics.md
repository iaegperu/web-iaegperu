The Committee seeks to create a space for professionals, researchers, educators, and students to discuss the ethical dimensions of geoscience research, education, and professional practice. It encourages consideration of environmental, social, cultural, and intergenerational responsibilities in decision-making related to natural hazards, infrastructure, geological resources, and territorial development.

## Mission and Vision

* Promote ethical principles, integrity, and accountability in geoscience practice.
* Encourage responsible approaches to research, education, consultancy, and public engagement.
* Foster dialogue on the social and environmental implications of engineering geology.
* Support inclusive and sustainable decision-making in the management of geological resources and territory.

## Initial Activities and Collaboration

The Committee is currently developing its initial programme of activities, including:

* Geoethics talks in our educational activities.
* Research and knowledge-exchange initiatives in geoethics.
* Collaboration with universities, professional associations, IAEG committees, and other interested institutions.

**The Geoethics Committee is actively seeking collaborators, researchers, educators, students, and institutions interested in developing research and joint initiatives in geoethics in Peru and the wider international community.**
