### **About the Committee**

Its mission is to communicate engineering geology and related geoscience topics accurately, accessibly and responsibly, while strengthening the visibility and institutional identity of IAEG Peru.

The Committee supports the communication of technical seminars, webinars, field activities, publications, projects and institutional initiatives. It helps connect academia, industry, public institutions, professional organizations, students and the wider community through clear, high-quality and scientifically sound communication.

Operating under a structured and accountable framework, the committee ensures seamless execution through centralized coordination with the Executive Committee. Operational success relies on deep collaboration with 
universities, professional associations, national institutions, international organizations and technical partners to expand the reach and impact of IAEG Peru initiatives.

The Committee operates through coordinated planning, defined responsibilities, shared timelines and close communication with the Executive Committee and other IAEG Peru committees.

Its work is based on collaboration among members with different technical, communication and organizational skills. The Committee supports the preparation and dissemination of accurate content, promotional materials, event communications, institutional announcements and public-facing resources.

**What We Develop**

The Committee may develop and support:

* Communication strategies for IAEG Peru initiatives
* Promotional campaigns for seminars, webinars, field trips and technical events
* Social media content and institutional announcements
* Visual materials, event graphics and multimedia resources
* Scientific and technical outreach content
* Communication support for publications, projects and partnerships
* Public relations and stakeholder-engagement activities
* Documentation and communication of institutional milestones
